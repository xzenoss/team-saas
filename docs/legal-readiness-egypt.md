# Egypt legal-readiness checklist

Status values: Not started, In review, Confirmed, or Not applicable.

This checklist is for counsel and an accountant. It is not a legal opinion.

## Company and tax

- [ ] Legal name and business purpose confirmed
- [ ] Founders agreement signed
- [ ] Company incorporated through GAFI
- [ ] Commercial registration issued
- [ ] Tax registration issued
- [ ] Company bank account opened
- [ ] ITIDA activity eligibility and license confirmed
- [ ] Electronic signature/seal requirements confirmed
- [ ] VAT position confirmed
- [ ] Electronic invoice and electronic receipt obligations confirmed
- [ ] Payroll and social-insurance obligations confirmed

## Data and security

- [ ] Data inventory completed
- [ ] Data controller/processor roles mapped
- [ ] Privacy notice approved in Arabic and English where needed
- [ ] Consent and lawful-basis records implemented
- [ ] Data retention and deletion schedule approved
- [ ] Data-subject request process implemented
- [ ] Vendor DPAs signed
- [ ] International transfer and hosting locations reviewed
- [ ] Security incident and breach-response process tested
- [ ] Encryption, key management, backups, access logging, and admin MFA enabled
- [ ] Personal Data Protection Law obligations and any required registrations or permits confirmed

## Messaging

- [ ] Product is classified as an application over third-party networks or a telecom service
- [ ] NTRA licensing position confirmed in writing
- [ ] SMS, voice, WhatsApp, email, and push providers are licensed or authorized as required
- [ ] Sender identity and consent rules documented
- [ ] Spam, abuse, blocking, and law-enforcement request process documented
- [ ] Message content and metadata retention limits approved

## Loyalty

- [ ] Points are legally defined as promotional benefits
- [ ] No cash redemption, deposit, lending, wallet, or payment function without separate approval
- [ ] Merchant funding and settlement contract approved
- [ ] Expiry, refunds, reversals, fraud, and account closure rules published
- [ ] Consumer disclosures and complaint handling approved
- [ ] CBE/FRA analysis completed if points can become stored value or a financial product

## AI

- [ ] Model vendors and processing locations documented
- [ ] Customer data use and training settings reviewed
- [ ] Human review required for high-impact outputs
- [ ] Prompt-injection and data-exfiltration tests completed
- [ ] Generated content, copyright, and user disclosure rules approved
- [ ] AI incident and rollback process tested

## IoT

- [ ] Device radio and communication components identified
- [ ] NTRA type-approval requirement confirmed for each model
- [ ] Import, manufacturing, customs, and product licensing requirements confirmed
- [ ] IoT service license requirement confirmed
- [ ] SIM and roaming arrangements use authorized operators
- [ ] Device identity, secure update, password change, factory reset, and revocation features implemented
- [ ] Hardware warranty, safety, repair, and recall process approved

## Evidence to keep

Keep regulator correspondence, approvals, contracts, consent records, security reviews, test results, invoices, device certificates, and change approvals in a controlled company repository.
