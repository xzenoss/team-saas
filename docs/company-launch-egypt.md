# Company launch roadmap — Egypt

This document is an operating checklist for building a technology company around trusted messaging, loyalty, AI, and IoT. It is not legal advice and does not replace an Egyptian lawyer, tax accountant, or regulator confirmation.

## Company shape

Start with one Egyptian technology company and separate product lines internally:

- Messaging and collaboration
- Merchant loyalty and rewards
- AI-assisted workflows
- IoT device management

Use a limited-liability or single-member structure only after the founders and Egyptian counsel confirm the ownership and governance choice. Keep product accounting, customer contracts, data flows, and risk registers separate even if they sit under one company.

## First 90 days

### Days 1–14 — decisions

- Confirm founders, ownership, management authority, and a written founders agreement.
- Select the legal name and reserve domains and social handles.
- Define the first customer and one paid use case.
- Decide that loyalty points are promotional points, not cash, deposits, wallets, credit, or payment instruments.
- Decide that messaging uses licensed third-party telecom/SMS/WhatsApp providers.
- Keep IoT in prototype and lab testing until device approvals and import requirements are confirmed.

### Days 15–30 — formation

- Incorporate through GAFI with an Egyptian corporate lawyer.
- Obtain commercial registration, tax registration, and the company bank account.
- Confirm the company activity codes and whether ITIDA licensing applies.
- Obtain qualified electronic signature/seal capability if needed for tax and government systems.
- Have an accountant confirm VAT, payroll, social insurance, invoicing, and electronic invoice/receipt obligations.

### Days 31–60 — trust foundation

- Inventory every category of personal data and where it is stored.
- Write privacy notice, terms of service, merchant agreement, DPA, acceptable-use policy, refund policy, and security policy.
- Add account deletion, data export, consent records, retention limits, audit logs, access reviews, backups, and incident response.
- Sign processor agreements with hosting, AI, messaging, analytics, and payment vendors.
- Threat-model account takeover, impersonation, spam, fraud, data leakage, and abusive automation.

### Days 61–90 — controlled pilot

- Pilot with a small number of Egyptian merchants and invited users.
- Use only promotional loyalty benefits with clear expiry and redemption terms.
- Measure delivery, abuse reports, retention, reward cost, AI errors, and support time.
- Get written NTRA advice before providing telecom services, importing communication devices, or operating IoT connectivity.
- Move from prototype to public launch only after counsel signs off on the exact product flows.

## Ownership of risk

The founder or board should maintain a written register for:

- Data protection
- Telecom and messaging
- Loyalty and financial activity
- IoT hardware and radio compliance
- AI output and content safety
- Tax and invoicing
- Intellectual property
- Employment and contractor agreements
- Vendor and cloud outages

Review this file whenever the product starts holding money, issuing cash-equivalent value, connecting regulated devices, or making decisions about people.
